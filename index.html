Rheindata

Customer app · student prototype, not a real bank product

The AI informs. The customer decides.

  1. Lena asks, like Akinator. Six or seven questions. The code guesses the customer's style (five archetypes): it only changes how Lena helps.
  2. Rules set the risk level. Defensive, Balanced or Opportunity, from the suitability answers, never from the AI. It decides the model portfolio shown (the same for everyone at that level) and the products open.
  3. AI on every ETF, in plain words. Trend, macro and news from our agents, with sources and the other side of the argument.
  4. The customer places each order. Rheindata is execution-only. Learning unlocks complex products; "decide for me" goes to the bank.
